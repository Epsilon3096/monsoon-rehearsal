# Fictional practice conversation — NOT operator validation

**Entirely invented for interview rehearsal. No person was interviewed. No testimonial, endorsement, date or professional approval is implied. Do not submit this as evidence.**

Interviewer: “Would comparing achievable work before rain be useful?”

Imagined reviewer: “It could help us explain why we chose one job over another. But I would first want to know whether your estimated clearance time includes access, removing the waste and getting the crew out. A job that looks quick on a map can take much longer in practice.”

Interviewer: “We give unfinished jobs no hydraulic benefit. Is that a reasonable assumption?”

Imagined reviewer: “Sometimes. It depends on what the crew has already disturbed. Temporary barriers or spoil can make conditions worse. You would need to agree a safe stopping condition for each type of work.”

Interviewer: “What would you want to see before trying it?”

Imagined reviewer: “Start with one surveyed drainage area. Let us record our normal decision before we see yours. Compare both against what actually happens, and keep the cases where your recommendation is wrong. I would treat the first trial as an observation exercise.”

These are prompts for critical questions, not findings. Replace them with a consented record of actual feedback; never simply remove this fictional label.
